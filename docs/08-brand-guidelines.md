# Brand guidelines

## Name and descriptor

Use **PCBuilderAI** exactly. Do not write PCBuilderAi, PC Builder AI, or PCBuilder.ai unless a future legal/domain decision requires it. Descriptor: “AI hardware consultant and PC community.”

## Positioning

PCBuilderAI is expert, transparent, calm, and practical. It should feel like a patient senior hardware consultant, not a hype-driven sales bot or retailer.

## Voice

- Lead with the recommendation or issue.
- Explain trade-offs in plain language, then expose technical depth.
- Be decisive when evidence is strong and explicit when it is not.
- Never shame a budget, brand preference, old component, or beginner question.
- Avoid guarantees about FPS, compatibility with unknown dimensions, live stock, and final tax.

## Ukrainian and English

Ukrainian is primary product authorship; English is separately edited, idiomatic copy. Maintain a bilingual terminology glossary for hardware roles, form factors, condition, moderation, and billing. Do not translate model names, standards, or merchant brands. Use locale-appropriate decimal/currency placement.

## Visual identity

The wordmark should be typographic and neutral; a future symbol may combine a modular grid/node motif with a build/check concept. Avoid clichéd robot heads, gaming flames, neon rainbow RGB, and motherboard-photo backgrounds. Accent color should communicate intelligence/precision and meet contrast in both themes.

## Imagery

Prefer high-quality component macro photography, clean system silhouettes, architectural diagrams, and subtle abstract computation patterns. Always secure usage rights and provide meaningful alt text; decorative images use empty alt.

## Product claims

Allowed: “checks compatibility,” “searches current offers,” “explains trade-offs.” Qualified: “estimated performance,” “estimated final price.” Prohibited: “guaranteed cheapest,” “100% compatible” when any fact is unknown, or “official retailer recommendation.”

## Required beta disclosure

Landing footer, About/legal context, and feedback surfaces state that PCBuilderAI is an independent author-created pet project in public beta, is not a retailer, and may produce incomplete estimates. This disclosure must be confident and unobtrusive, not apologetic.
