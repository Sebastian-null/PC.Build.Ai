## Next delivery milestones

1. **Gemini live AI:** streaming, multimodal uploads, grounded Search/URL/File tools, citations, requirement extraction, context, regenerate/continue/copy/share, chat search/favorites.
2. **Hardware and market truth:** normalized component/evidence/merchant/price tables, direct product URLs, verified availability, USD/UAH, USA sales tax/ZIP, Ukrainian VAT, shipping and assembly, used/refurbished/discontinued modes.
3. **Compatibility engine:** socket/chipset/BIOS, RAM generation/speed/ECC, cooler and case clearance, GPU dimensions, PSU wattage/connectors, storage and PCIe topology, deterministic blocker/warning/unknown outcomes.
4. **Build workspace:** immutable versions, alternatives, upgrade paths, performance/FPS/workstation estimates with sources, privacy/share, collaboration, unlimited comparisons and optional AI summary.
5. **Community:** bilingual forum sections, categories/topics/posts/comments, build showcases, likes, bookmarks, search/filter, automatic translation and public user profiles.
6. **Moderation:** pre-publication word/insult checks, draft preservation, reports, hidden review, 1/3/7-day timeouts, 182.5-day strike expiry, fourth-strike ban, appeals and super-admin console.
7. **Notifications:** in-app, browser push and opt-in email by event type.
8. **Billing:** five completed generations per rolling 24-hour window; Stripe USD 20/month, one-day one-time trial, seven-day unconditional refund, fair-use protection.
9. **Account/privacy:** settings, exports, deletion/anonymization, 24-hour deleted-chat recovery, granular consent and activity/statistics.
10. **Production hardening:** tests, accessibility, analytics/monitoring, security, performance, deployment and legal placeholders.

## Data disclaimer

Current store cards are deliberately labeled demo offers and link to retailer search. Direct verified product listings, availability, real images from cited product sources, and current prices require the Gemini grounded-search milestone and merchant verification pipeline. The UI must never present demo or stale data as live inventory.
