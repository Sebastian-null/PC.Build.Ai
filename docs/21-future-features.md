# Future features

Future items require discovery and are not represented as functional beta controls.

## Market intelligence

- Historical price charts, price-drop alerts, discount authenticity, stock alerts
- Official retailer/feed adapters and affiliate disclosures
- More countries, currencies, tax/shipping calculators, localized merchant trust models
- Saved offer watchlists and purchase timing guidance

## Hardware intelligence

- Richer thermals/acoustics and physical layout visualization
- Upgrade-path simulations over time and total cost of ownership
- Electricity-cost and carbon estimates by region
- Firmware/recall notices and warranty comparisons
- Advanced homelab/server topology, storage resiliency, networking and rack planning

## Community

- Reputation, badges, verified experts, achievements
- Delegated moderator/support roles and granular RBAC
- Community compatibility evidence proposals with expert review
- Events, build challenges, curated guides and collections

## Collaboration and business

- Team workspaces, client proposals, comments on individual component rows
- Annual/team subscriptions, gift plans, educational programs
- Embeddable public build cards and exports to retailer carts where agreements allow

## Experience

- Additional human-authored locales
- Installable PWA/offline saved builds
- Voice input/read-aloud and richer accessible comparison narration
- More advanced semantic search after PostgreSQL limits are reached

## Explicit exclusions

Direct messages are excluded from beta and need a safety/moderation case before reconsideration. Multiple AI providers are not planned. Laptops are explicitly outside product scope and require a new product decision, data model, UX, and evaluation set—not an incremental toggle.

## Future-feature gate

Every candidate must state user problem, success/guardrail metrics, privacy/safety effect, localization work, cost, migration path, operational ownership, and rollback. Historical data cannot be promised retroactively unless beta snapshots have sufficient quality and legal provenance.
