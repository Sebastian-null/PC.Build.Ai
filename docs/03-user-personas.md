# User personas

## 1. First-time builder — “Ira”

Has a strict budget and gaming goals but little vocabulary. Needs guided questions, explanations, confidence, and warnings without intimidation. Success: understands why each part exists and obtains a build within budget. Risks: confusing subtotal with checkout total or treating estimated FPS as guaranteed.

## 2. Value optimizer — “Alex”

Knows brands and benchmarks, wants best price/performance, is willing to buy used, and challenges recommendations. Needs sources, condition toggles, alternatives, comparison, and editable constraints. Success: can audit every claim and see marginal cost/performance.

## 3. Creator/streamer — “Marta”

Balances gaming, encoding, editing, storage, noise, and reliability. Needs workload weighting, application-specific benchmark evidence, accelerator/codec considerations, memory/storage planning, and quiet operation.

## 4. Workstation/server buyer — “Danylo”

Needs ECC/virtualization, capacity, lane topology, network/storage options, reliability, and lifecycle transparency. May accept used/discontinued server parts after warnings. Success: compatibility includes platform-specific topology, not only consumer socket matching.

## 5. Enthusiast collaborator — “Sam”

Creates several variants and collaborates with friends/clients. Needs version history, comments/activity, unlimited comparison, invitations, conflict safety, and polished sharing.

## 6. Community contributor

Publishes showcases, answers questions, bookmarks references, and participates across English/Ukrainian sections. Needs discoverability, translation, fair moderation, draft preservation, attribution, and notification control.

## 7. Super-admin / owner

Single operator responsible for reports, strikes, bans, appeals, categories, user safety, billing visibility, feature controls, audit review, and operational health. Needs queues prioritized by harm/age, reversible actions, explicit reason codes, and protection against accidental destructive actions.

## Accessibility and situational personas

All personas may use keyboard-only navigation, screen readers, zoom, reduced motion, low-bandwidth mobile networks, or non-native language settings. These are baseline requirements rather than edge cases.

## Jobs to be done

- “When I state goals in ordinary language, help me discover missing constraints and make a defensible purchase plan.”
- “When a recommendation changes, show exactly why, what improved, and what trade-off appeared.”
- “When prices are volatile, tell me when and where facts were observed.”
- “When I publish, let others understand and discuss the exact version I meant.”
- “When moderation blocks me, preserve my work and tell me how to correct or appeal.”
